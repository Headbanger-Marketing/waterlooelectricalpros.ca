Enquiry privacy notice.

Published October 8, 2026

Who receives your information?

The enquiry team receives enquiries submitted through waterlooelectricalpros.ca. The enquiry form sends the details you enter, the source page, project category and submission time to the enquiry intake system.

Why do we collect it?

We use your name, email, phone, city, postal code and project description, together with any street address or work selection you provide, to review your request, contact you about it, and assess a potential service provider introduction. When you consent, relevant details may be shared with a potential provider for that assessment. A provider engaged by you handles information under its own practices.

Your choices

Submitting a project enquiry is voluntary. Avoid sensitive personal information in the description. You may request access, correction, or deletion of enquiry details by emailing contact@waterlooelectricalpros.ca. Tell us which enquiry you mean without including identity documents. We may need to verify the request before responding.

Storage and service providers

Enquiries may be handled using hosted workflow, email and record systems used by the enquiry team. These services process details needed to receive and review your request. Information may be processed outside your province or country. We keep information for enquiry handling and related business record needs; contact us to ask about your specific record.

Automated Assistant

Your chat messages are processed by the enquiry team and Anthropic to provide automated answers. If you choose to submit an enquiry and agree to the consent notice, the enquiry team records your contact details, property location and request, and forwards them to a relevant service provider. Coverage, quotes and appointments require separate confirmation. Do not send payment details or other sensitive information through chat.

Website analytics and external links

This site does not install visitor analytics, advertising pixels or marketing tracking cookies. Hosting services may retain routine security and delivery logs. Links to ESA and other third parties are governed by their own privacy practices.

Contact

Send privacy questions to contact@waterlooelectricalpros.ca, identifying this domain in the subject.
